What is the dress code for the symposium?
The dress code is business casual. Formal attire is not required, but please avoid overly casual clothing such as flip-flops or beachwear.

Is there food provided at the event?
Yes, lunch is provided on both days. Vegetarian and vegan options are available at every meal station. Please inform the registration desk in advance if you have specific dietary restrictions or allergies.

Can I bring a guest who is not registered?
Guests must register separately through the same portal. Walk-ins are only permitted if seats are available and will be charged a small on-site fee.

Is parking available at the venue?
Yes, free parking is available in Lot B, adjacent to the main building. Lot A is reserved for speakers and staff.

Will sessions be recorded?
Most sessions will be recorded and made available to registered attendees within one week after the symposium. A few workshop sessions are not recorded due to speaker preference.

What should I do if I lose my badge?
Visit the registration desk in the main lobby. A replacement badge can be issued after verifying your registration details.

Is there Wi-Fi available?
Yes, free Wi-Fi is available throughout the venue. The network name and password are printed on the back of your badge.
