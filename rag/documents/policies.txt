Cancellation Policy
Registrations can be cancelled up to 5 days before the event for a full refund. Cancellations within 5 days of the event are non-refundable but can be transferred to another attendee.

Code of Conduct
All attendees, speakers, and staff are expected to treat each other with respect. Harassment of any kind will not be tolerated and may result in removal from the event without refund.

Photography and Recording Policy
Photography for personal use is allowed in common areas. Recording of sessions by attendees is not permitted; official recordings will be shared after the event where available.

Accessibility
The venue is wheelchair accessible, including all session rooms and restrooms. Attendees needing additional accommodations should contact the organizing team at least one week in advance.

Certificate of Participation
Certificates are issued digitally within two weeks after the symposium to all attendees who completed check-in on the day of the event.
